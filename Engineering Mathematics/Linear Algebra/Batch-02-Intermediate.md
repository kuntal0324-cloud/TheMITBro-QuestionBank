## LA-MAT-031

**Difficulty:** Intermediate  
**Type:** MCQ  
**Marks:** 1

A = [ 2  1
      3  2 ]

then det(A⁻¹) is

A. 1

B. -1

C. 2

D. -2

**Answer:** B

**Concept Tested:** Determinant of inverse matrix.
